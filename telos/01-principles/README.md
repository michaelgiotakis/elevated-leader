# 01 — Principles

This section documents the founding logic, values, and design philosophy that govern every business decision across The Elevated Leader ecosystem.

## What belongs here

- The core mission and purpose of the business
- The founding belief system and philosophical pillars
- Brand and editorial values (the "why" behind every design and operational choice)
- The principles that define how the business treats clients, creates offers, and shows up in the world
- Non-negotiables: things the business will never do and why
- The emotional territory the business occupies and defends

## Why this exists

Principles are the upstream constraint for every downstream decision. When a pipeline stage, automation, or communication choice feels wrong, the answer lives here.
