# 02 — Organisation

This section documents how The Elevated Leader is structured as a business: its operating model, roles, responsibilities, and the logic behind how work is organised.

## What belongs here

- Organisational structure and reporting lines
- Role definitions and accountabilities
- Operating model overview (how the business runs day-to-day)
- Founder role and involvement model
- Contractor, team, and partner relationships
- Decision rights and authority levels
- How the business scales without dependency on the founder

## Why this exists

Clarity on structure prevents ambiguity in execution. This section ensures every person working within the ecosystem understands their mandate and how their work connects to the whole.
