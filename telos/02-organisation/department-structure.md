# Department Structure

This document defines the approved departments of The Elevated Leader and the ownership boundary of each. It is the authoritative reference for organisational structure and accountability.

---

## Approved Departments

1. Executive
2. Marketing
3. Sales
4. Delivery and Coaching
5. Finance
6. Administration
7. Operations
8. Technology and AI

---

## Department Ownership Boundaries

### 1. Executive

Executive sets direction, priorities, budgets, and company-level measures.

The Executive department is accountable for:
- Business strategy and vision
- Prioritisation of initiatives and investment
- Budget ownership
- Company-level KPIs and performance standards
- Final approval on locked TELOS documents

---

### 2. Marketing

Marketing owns brand, market research, content, website strategy, web design direction, funnels, sales pages, lead generation, diagnostic promotion, webinar marketing, advertising, attribution requirements, and pre-sale communication.

Marketing is accountable for:
- Brand identity and its consistent expression
- Editorial content and content strategy
- Website strategy and design direction (not code — see Technology)
- Sales funnel strategy and customer-facing funnel assets
- Sales page strategy and copy
- Lead generation channels and campaigns
- Diagnostic tool promotion
- Webinar and event marketing
- Advertising campaigns and performance
- Attribution requirements (what the business needs to track about lead sources)
- All communication before a lead becomes an active sales opportunity

> Marketing owns funnel strategy, customer-facing funnel assets, and web design direction. Operations owns the CRM and workflow architecture supporting those funnels. Technology owns the implementation requiring code or infrastructure.

---

### 3. Sales

Sales receives and manages active opportunities, performs outreach, records interactions, updates opportunities, conducts sales conversations, and closes deals.

Sales is accountable for:
- Receiving qualified leads and opportunities from Marketing pipelines
- Conducting discovery and sales conversations
- Recording all interactions and outcomes in the CRM
- Updating opportunity stages accurately and promptly
- Managing the relationship from qualified lead through to closed deal
- Revenue conversion performance

> Operations builds and maintains the machine. Sales manages the opportunities and activities given to it.

---

### 4. Delivery and Coaching

Delivery and Coaching owns product access, communities, course delivery, coaching delivery, client progress, and customer outcomes.

Delivery and Coaching is accountable for:
- Granting and managing client access to programmes and communities
- Delivering coaching sessions and group programmes
- Monitoring client progress and engagement
- Managing the client experience from enrolment through to completion
- Tracking and improving client outcomes
- Maintaining community health and engagement

---

### 5. Finance

Finance owns revenue, expenses, reconciliation, forecasting, refunds, taxes, and financial reporting.

Finance is accountable for:
- Revenue tracking and reconciliation against Stripe data
- Expense management and approval
- Financial forecasting and cash flow visibility
- Processing refunds within policy
- Tax compliance and reporting
- Financial dashboards and period reporting

---

### 6. Administration

Administration owns general administration, internal records, support coordination, documentation support, and people administration.

Administration is accountable for:
- Internal record-keeping and file management
- Coordinating customer support requests to the appropriate department
- Supporting documentation maintenance within TELOS
- People administration including contractors and onboarding logistics

---

### 7. Operations

Operations designs, governs, segments, and maintains the CRM, pipelines, deal stages, tags, fields, workflows, automations, reporting structure, process architecture, and operating documentation.

Operations is accountable for:
- CRM architecture design and governance
- Pipeline design, stage definitions, and entry and exit criteria
- Tag taxonomy and field architecture
- Workflow and automation design and documentation
- Reporting structure and dashboard design
- Naming conventions and data standards
- TELOS documentation and version control
- Process architecture across all departments
- Coordinating implementation with Technology when code or infrastructure is required

> Operations builds and maintains the machine. Sales manages the opportunities and activities given to it.

> Marketing owns funnel strategy, customer-facing funnel assets, and web design direction. Operations owns the CRM and workflow architecture supporting those funnels. Technology owns the implementation requiring code or infrastructure.

---

### 8. Technology and AI

Technology and AI owns website code, hosting, APIs, technical integrations, webhooks requiring development, custom software, security, infrastructure, and AI systems.

Technology and AI is accountable for:
- Website development and maintenance (Next.js, Vercel)
- DNS management and security (Cloudflare)
- Technical integrations requiring code or API configuration
- Webhooks requiring custom development
- Infrastructure and hosting architecture
- Security policies and access controls
- Custom software and automation scripts
- AI system design, deployment, and governance
