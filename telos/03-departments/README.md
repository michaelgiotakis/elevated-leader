# 03 — Departments

This section defines the functional areas of The Elevated Leader and the mandate of each.

## What belongs here

- Department or functional area definitions (e.g. Delivery, Growth, Operations, Content, Technology)
- The purpose and mandate of each functional area
- Key responsibilities per department
- How departments interact and hand off to one another
- Metrics each department owns
- Current state vs. future state of each functional area

## Why this exists

Functional clarity allows the business to operate modularly. Even as a small or founder-led operation, thinking in departments creates the scaffolding for clean delegation and eventual scale.
