# 04 — Capabilities

This section maps the core competencies and capabilities required to deliver on the promise of The Elevated Leader ecosystem.

## What belongs here

- Capability map across the full business model
- Current capabilities vs. required capabilities
- Capability gaps and how they will be filled (hire, train, partner, automate)
- IP and proprietary methodologies the business owns
- Signature frameworks and their relationship to business capabilities
- How capabilities are protected, developed, and transferred

## Why this exists

Capabilities are the engine of differentiation. This section ensures the business knows what it is genuinely excellent at, what it needs to build, and what it should never outsource.
