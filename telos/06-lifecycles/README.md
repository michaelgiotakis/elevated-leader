# 06 — Lifecycles

This section maps the complete lifecycle of key entities in the business — from first contact to completion, renewal, or exit.

## What belongs here

- Client lifecycle: awareness → enquiry → enrolment → delivery → completion → alumni → re-engagement
- Offer lifecycle: concept → development → launch → delivery → retirement
- Content lifecycle: ideation → creation → publication → distribution → archiving
- Lead lifecycle: cold → warm → qualified → converted → inactive
- Stage definitions, transition criteria, and what happens at each gate

## Why this exists

Lifecycles reveal where clients get stuck, where offers lose momentum, and where operational breakdowns occur. Mapping them is a prerequisite for designing pipelines and automations that actually reflect how the business works.
