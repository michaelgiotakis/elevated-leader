# 09 — CRM

This section documents the CRM architecture, contact model, segmentation logic, and record management standards for The Elevated Leader.

## What belongs here

- CRM platform decision and rationale
- Contact object structure and custom field definitions
- Contact segmentation model (how contacts are categorised and tagged)
- Lead source tracking and attribution model
- List architecture and membership rules
- Record hygiene standards (deduplication, data ownership, update protocols)
- Integration map: how the CRM connects to other systems
- CRM ownership and access controls

## Why this exists

The CRM is the memory of the business. Every client relationship, every sales conversation, every delivery touchpoint passes through it. A poorly designed CRM creates noise. A well-designed one creates leverage.
