# 10 — Pipelines

This section defines all sales, delivery, and operational pipelines — their stages, entry criteria, exit criteria, and logic.

## What belongs here

- Pipeline inventory (every named pipeline in the business)
- Stage-by-stage definitions for each pipeline
- Entry criteria: what must be true for a record to enter each stage
- Exit criteria: what triggers progression or removal
- Ownership: who is responsible for moving records through each pipeline
- Pipeline health metrics (conversion rates, average stage duration, drop-off points)
- How pipelines connect to automations and communications

## Why this exists

Pipelines make the invisible visible. Without defined stages and criteria, it is impossible to know where leads or clients actually are, what needs to happen next, or where the business is losing momentum.
