# 11 — Automations

This section is the canonical record of every automation workflow across the business — what triggers it, what it does, and what system runs it.

## What belongs here

- Automation inventory (every named automation workflow)
- Trigger definition (event, schedule, or condition that starts the automation)
- Action sequence (step-by-step description of what happens)
- Conditions and branching logic
- System ownership (which tool runs this automation)
- Integration dependencies (which systems must be connected for it to work)
- Error handling and fallback behaviour
- Testing and verification status

## Why this exists

Undocumented automations are a liability. They break silently, produce unexpected behaviour, and cannot be improved without being understood first. Every automation that runs in this business must be recorded here.
