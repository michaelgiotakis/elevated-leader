# 12 — Communications

This section documents the communication architecture of The Elevated Leader — every channel, sequence, template, and messaging framework.

## What belongs here

- Channel strategy (email, SMS, community, direct message, social)
- Email sequence library (onboarding, nurture, sales, re-engagement, delivery)
- Template standards and brand voice guidelines for written communication
- Subject line frameworks and naming conventions
- Communication cadence map (when clients and leads hear from the business and why)
- Transactional vs. relational communication distinctions
- Unsubscribe and consent management logic
- Communication ownership (who writes, approves, and sends each type)

## Why this exists

Communication is the primary relationship surface of the business. Undocumented sequences drift off-brand, create inconsistent client experiences, and are impossible to optimise without a baseline. Every touchpoint belongs here.
