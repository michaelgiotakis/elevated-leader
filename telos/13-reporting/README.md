# 13 — Reporting

This section defines the metrics, dashboards, KPIs, and reporting cadences used to measure the health and performance of the business.

## What belongs here

- North Star metric and its definition
- KPI hierarchy (business-level, department-level, pipeline-level)
- Dashboard inventory and what each dashboard shows
- Reporting cadence (daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly reviews)
- Metric definitions (sourced from the Data Dictionary)
- Who reviews which reports and what decisions they should inform
- Leading vs. lagging indicator classifications
- Benchmarks and target ranges for key metrics

## Why this exists

A business without clear metrics is navigating blind. This section ensures the right people are looking at the right numbers at the right time — and that those numbers mean the same thing to everyone who reads them.
