# 15 — Governance

This section documents how decisions are made, how the operating system is maintained, and how change is managed across The Elevated Leader.

## What belongs here

- Decision-making framework (who decides what, and how)
- TELOS update protocol (how changes to this operating system are proposed, reviewed, and applied)
- Review cadences (when each section of TELOS is reviewed and by whom)
- Change log standards (how changes are recorded in the CHANGELOG)
- Ownership matrix (who is the named owner of each section)
- Escalation paths for unresolved decisions
- How new initiatives are evaluated against existing principles
- Onboarding standards for new team members and contractors

## Why this exists

Without governance, operating systems decay. Good work gets undone, inconsistencies accumulate, and the business slowly drifts from its own principles. This section ensures TELOS remains a living system, not a static document.
